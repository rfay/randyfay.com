Add-another and node form

Hi Jeff-

Add another works just like the examples in the Examples project: Use a AHAH-enabled submit button, and build the form with another field. Essentially, you're driving it with a submit instead of a select or checkbox like in the example.

The D7 AJAX example has an explicit example of add-more - You may want to roll it for D6 and we'll add it to the Examples.
